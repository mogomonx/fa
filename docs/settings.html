Colours

Colours are saved in your browser, so they're personal to this device.

On This Date

View any list as it stood on a past date: Records, Event Rankings, Sum of Ranks/Kinch, Individual Results, Top 100, Historical Records, Record Age, Streaks, Compare and profiles all recompute from that date. Recent Activity, Upcoming Competitions, Rolling Averages, PR Counting and Consistency stay live regardless. Saved in your browser and applied to every list you open until you clear it.

Time frame

Only count results from competitions inside a start and end date, for example just 2026. Records, Event Rankings, Sum of Ranks/Kinch, Individual Results, Top 100, Compare and profiles use only results from inside the frame. PR Streaks and Historical Records judge PRs and records against everything before the end date, but only show the ones set inside the frame. Set a start, an end, or both; if On This Date is also set, the earlier end date wins. Recent Activity, Upcoming Competitions, Rolling Averages, PR Counting and Consistency stay live. Saved in your browser.
